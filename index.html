    About

    I am a PhD student at Cnam Paris working on network intrusion detection, supported by the French ANR GNADIS project (ANR-23-IAS4-0002). My research asks a practical question: when does modelling traffic as a graph actually help detect attacks — and when is a well-tuned classical model enough?

    I work on temporal graph attention models for IoT attack detection, on benchmarking NLP-style and tabular transformers for intrusion detection, and on detecting the stages of Advanced Persistent Threats. I care about reproducibility, leakage auditing and calibrated, explainable models.

    Before Cnam, I graduated as a computer science engineer in intelligent systems & data science from ESI Algiers. I also teach web development (HTML/CSS) and computer networks, and supervise master's students on IoT attack detection.
